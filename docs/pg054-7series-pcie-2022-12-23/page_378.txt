PIPETXnPOWERDOWN[1:0]
Output
PIPECLK
This output is the Power Management signal for the 
transmitter for lane n:
• 00b: P0 (Normal operation)
• 01b: P0s (Low recovery time power-saving state)
• 10b: P1 (Longer recovery time power state)
• 11b: Reserved
