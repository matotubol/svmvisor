1.6
Changes Between Revisions and Product Variations
1.6.1
Revision Conventions
The processor revision is specified by CPUID_Fn00000001_EAX (FamModStep) or CPUID_Fn80000001_EAX 
(FamModStepExt). This document uses a revision letter instead of specific model numbers. Where applicable, the 
processor stepping is indicated after the revision letter. All behavior marked with a revision letter apply to future revisions 
unless they are superseded by a change in a later revision. See the revision guide in 1.2 [Reference Documents] for 
additional information about revision determination.
