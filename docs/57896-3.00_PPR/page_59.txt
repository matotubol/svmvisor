Read-only. Reset: 0000_0000h. 
The interrupt request registers provide a bit per interrupt to indicate that the corresponding interrupt has been accepted 
by the APIC. The first 16 RequestBits of the first Core::X86::Apic::InterruptRequest register are Reserved.
