2.3.3    SSE Instructions
Registers.   The SSE instructions operate primarily on 128-bit, 256-bit, and 512-bit floating-point
vector operands located in the 512-bit ZMM/YMM/XMM registers. Each 128-bit XMM register is
defined as the lower octword of the corresponding YMM register. Each 256-bit YMM register is
defined as the lower two octwords of the corresponding ZMM register. The number of available
ZMM/YMM/XMM data registers depends on the operating mode, as shown in Figure 2-8 below. In
legacy and compatibility modes, eight ZMM/YMM/XMM registers (ZMM/YMM/XMM0–7) are
available. In 64-bit mode, twenty four additional ZMM/YMM/XMM data registers
(ZMM/YMM/XMM8–31) are available. These eight additional registers are addressed via the
encoding extensions provided by the REX, VEX, XOP, and EVEX prefixes.
