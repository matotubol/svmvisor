15.13.4  INIT Intercept
The INIT intercept allows the VMM to intercept the assertion of INIT while a guest is running. An 
intercepted INIT remains pending until the VMM sets GIF (see “Global Interrupt Flag, STGI and 
CLGI Instructions” on page 530), at which point it either takes effect or is redirected. See 
Section 15.21.8 for a discussion of the INIT redirection feature.
