13.1.1.7  Control-Transfer Recording MSRs
Figure 13-5 on page 399 shows the format of the 64-bit control-transfer recording MSRs: 
LastBranchToIP, LastBranchFromIP, LastIntToIP, and LastIntFromIP. These registers are loaded 
automatically by the processor when the DebugCtl[LBR] bit is set to 1. These MSRs are read-only.
