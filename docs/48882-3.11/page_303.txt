Assigned DeviceID Device Entries
Device entries for devices with assigned device IDs are fixed in length. Two formats are defined: 4-
byte and 8-byte.
IVHD 4-byte device entries
The 4-byte IVHD device entry is structured to contain a single DeviceID with related DTE settings. 
• For a 4-byte entry, use a length of 4 bytes to locate the next entry.
• Similarly, for an 8-byte entry, use a length of 8 bytes to locate the next entry.
