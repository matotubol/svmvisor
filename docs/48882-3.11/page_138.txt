When IOMMU HW detects a vIOMMU fault, the IOMMU might decide to halt the vIOMMU com-
mand processing (by clearing the vCmdRun) or the whole vIOMMU functionality (by clearing both 
vCmdRun and vIommuRun). SW must first clear the corresponding vIommuEn/vCmdEn bit(s) then

Before resuming operations for a particular Guest after the RESET_VMMIO command was issued, 
SW must reprogram the appropriate Guest VF Control registers.
