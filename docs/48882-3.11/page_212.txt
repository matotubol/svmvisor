AMD I/O Virtualization Technology (IOMMU) Specification
This register specifies the ID and the Revision of the IOMMU Vendor Specific Capability Block.
The registers specified below are the base address (SPA) of the IOMMU Virtual Function MMIO 
area.   The area must be 256M aligned.
