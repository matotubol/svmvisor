AMD I/O Virtualization Technology (IOMMU) Specification
This register specifies the limit of the IOMMU exclusion range. The lower 12 bits of the limit are 
treated as FFFh for range comparisons.
Note: When the exclusion base address equals the exclusion limit address, the exclusion range is 
4 KB.

Exclusion range limit. RW. Reset 00_0000_0000h. Specifies bits [51:12] of the upper limit of the exclusion

the Completion store limit field. Bits 11:0 of the completion store limit address are treated as F_FFh. The 
completion store limit address defines the upper address range that host COMPLETION_WAIT stores may
