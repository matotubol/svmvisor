5.2.12.17 GIC Redistributor (GICR) Structure
The GICR Structure enables the discovery of GIC Redistributor base addresses by providing the Physical Base Address
of a page range containing the GIC Redistributors. More than one GICR Structure may be presented in the MADT.
GICR structures should only be used when describing GIC implementations which conform to version 3 or higher
of the GIC architecture and which place all Redistributors in the always-on power domain.When a GICR structure is
presented, the OSPM must ignore the GICR Base Address field of the GICC structures.
