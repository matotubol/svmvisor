5.2.19.1 Maximum Proximity Domain Information Structure
The Maximum Proximity Domain Information Structure is used to report system maximum characteristics. It is likely
that these characteristics may be the same for many proximity domains, but they can vary from one proximity domain
to another. This structure optimizes to cover the former case, while allowing the flexibility for the latter as well. These
structures must be organized in ascending order of the proximity domain enumerations. All proximity domains within
the Maximum Number of Proximity Domains reported in the MSCT must be covered by one of these structures.
