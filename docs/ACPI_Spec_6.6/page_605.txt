8.4.3.2.2.1 OS Initiated Request Semantics
With OS Initiated coordination, the ordering of requests from different cores is critically important since the platform
acts upon the latest one. If the platform does not process requests in the order the OS intended then it may put the
platform into the wrong state. Consider this scenario in our example system in Power states for processor hierarchy,
as shown in the following table.
