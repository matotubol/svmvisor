5.2.12.30 RISC-V Platform Level Interrupt Controller (PLIC) Structure
The RISC-V Platform-Level Interrupt Controller Specification defines a platform level interrupt controller (PLIC) for
handling wired interrupts in a RISC-V platform. A PLIC signals an interrupt to a hart through a dedicated connection,
usually a wire, for each privilege level that the hart may receive interrupts. A system may contain multiple PLICs with
each PLIC handling interrupts from a different subset of devices and signaling a different subset of harts. Every PLIC
exposed to OSPM must have a matching MADT PLIC structure defined.
