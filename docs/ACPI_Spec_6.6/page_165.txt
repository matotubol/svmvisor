4.8.4.2.3 Fan
ACPI has a device driver to control fans (active cooling devices) in platforms. A fan is defined as a device with the
Plug and Play ID of “PNP0C0B.” It should then contain a list power resources used to control the fan.
For more information, see ACPI-Defined Devices and Device-Specific Objects.
