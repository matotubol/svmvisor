5.2.30.2 PDTT PCC Trigger Order
The trigger order defines two categories for triggers
Trigger Order 0: Triggers are invoked by OSPM before executing its crash dump processing functions.
Trigger Order 1: Triggers are invoked by OSPM at the end of crash dump processing functions, typically after the
kernel has processed crash dumps.
Capturing platform specific debug information from certain IPs would require intrusive mechanism which may limit
kernel operations after the operations. Trigger order allows the platform to define such operations that will be invoked
at the end of kernel operations by OSPM.
