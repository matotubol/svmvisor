There can be a catenation of one or more of these EFI_PEI_PPI_DESCRIPTORs. The final descriptor will have
the EFI_PEI_PPI_DESCRIPTOR_TERMINATE_LIST flag set to indicate to the PEI Foundation how many of the
descriptors need to be added to the PPI database within the PEI Foundation. The PEI Services that references
this data structure include InstallPpi() , ReinstallPpi() , and LocatePpi() .
