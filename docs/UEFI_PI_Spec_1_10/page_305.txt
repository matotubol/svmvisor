II-9.7.2.4 GetTime()
When the DXE Foundation is notified that the EFI_REAL_TIME_CLOCK_ARCH_PROTOCOL has been installed, then
the Runtime Service GetTime() is available. The DXE driver that produces the EFI_REAL_TIME_CLOCK_ARCH_
PROTOCOL is responsible for directly updating the GetTime field of the UEFI Runtime Services Table. The DXE
Foundation is only responsible for updating the 32-bit CRC of the UEFI Runtime Services Table.
