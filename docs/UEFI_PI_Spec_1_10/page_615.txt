III-8.1.1 PCD Protocol
III-8.1.1.1 EFI_PCD_PROTOCOL
Summary
A platform database that contains a variety of current platform settings or directives that can be accessed by a
driver or application.
GUID
#define EFI_PCD_PROTOCOL_GUID \
