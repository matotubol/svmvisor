UEFI Platform Initialization Specification, Release 1.10
I-6.8 Firmware Volumes
Pre-EFI Initialization Modules (PEIMs) reside in firmware volumes (FVs). The PEI phase supports the ability
for PEIMs to reside in multiple firmware volumes.. Other PEIMs can expose firmware volumes for use by the
PEI Foundation.
