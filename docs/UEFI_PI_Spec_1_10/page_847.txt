Description
This function returns the vector information for the vector specified by Arg2 . If the specified vector was not
previously registered with the SAL Procedure SAL_SET_VECTORS , then SAL_NO_INFORMATION_AVAILABLE
is returned. Otherwise, the physical address of the requested vector is returned in SAL_RETURN_REGS . r9 ,
the global pointer(GP) value is returned in SAL_RETURN_REGS . r10 , the length and checksum information
is returned in SAL_RETURN_REGS . r10 , and EFI_SUCCESS is returned.
