III-3.2.1.2 EFI Signed Firmware Volumes
There may be one or more headers with a FormatType of value EFI_FIRMWARE_CONTENTS_SIGNED_GUID.
A signed firmware volume is a cryptographic signature across the entire volume. To process the contents and
verify the integrity of the volume, the EFI_FIRMWARE_VOLUME_EXT_ENTRY_GUID_TYPE Data[] shall contain an
instance of WIN_CERTIFICATE_UEFI_GUID where the CertType = EFI_CERT_TYPE_PKCS7_GUID, EFI_CERT_
TYPE_RSA2048_SHA256_GUID, EFI_CERT_TYPE_RSA3072_SHA384_GUID, EFI_CERT_TYPE_RSA4096_
SHA512_GUID, EFI_CERT_TYPE_RSAPSS3072_SHA512_GUID, EFI_CERT_TYPE_RSAPSS4096_SHA512_
GUID, EFI_CERT_TYPE_ECDSA_ECC_NIST_P256_SHA256_GUID, or EFI_CERT_TYPE_ECDSA_ECC_NIST_
P384_SHA384_GUID.
