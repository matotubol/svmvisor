I-12.3.2 SEC Platform Information 2 PPI (Optional)
I-12.3.2.1 EFI_SEC_PLATFORM_INFORMATION2_PPI (Optional)
Summary
This service is the primary handoff state into the PEI Foundation. The Security (SEC) component creates the
early, transitory memory environment and also encapsulates knowledge of at least the location of the Boot
Firmware Volume (BFV).

PlatformInformation for implementations that synchronize some, if not all CPU’s in the SEC phase.
